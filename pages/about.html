Who We Are

More Than an
Event Company

Rangify Decorz was founded with one unwavering belief: every celebration deserves to be extraordinary — regardless of budget. We saw a gap in the region between generic local event setups and the premium, stress-free experience that families and businesses truly deserve.

We built Rangify Decorz from the ground up — with our own team, our own materials, and our own standards. Today, we are proud to be the most trusted end-to-end event management company in Unnao, Kanpur, and Lucknow.
